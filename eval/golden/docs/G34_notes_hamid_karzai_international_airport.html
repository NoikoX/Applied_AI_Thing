Hamid Karzai International Airport

Hamid Karzai International Airport (Persian: میدان هوائی بین المللی حامدکرزی‎ ‎ , Pashto: د حامدکرزی نړيوال هوائي ډګر‎ , IATA: KBL, ICAO: OAKB ), is located 5 km from the city center of Kabul in Afghanistan. It serves as one of the nation's main international airports and as one of the largest military bases, capable of housing over one hundred aircraft. It was previously named Kabul International Airport and locally as Khwaja Rawash Airport, though it continues to be officially known by some airliners by the latter name. The airport was given its new name in 2014 in honor of former President Hamid Karzai. The decision was made by the National Assembly of Afghanistan and the Cabinet of President Ashraf Ghani.
